      Questions

      Good to know

      What is UniVersity?

      A free planner for choosing a university. You search and compare universities in 15 countries, check your chances with your own grades, see what it really costs to live there, and keep every application and scholarship deadline in one timeline.

      Do I need an account to try it?

      No. The search and city guide on this page work without one. You only need a (free) account to save universities, add grades and track deadlines.

      How are my chances worked out?

      We compare your grade with how selective each university is and place it as a reach, a match or a safety. It’s an estimate — it can’t see essays, interviews or course quotas — so we always say so.

      Where do the numbers come from?

      Tuition and city living costs come from real published data. Salaries, scholarship odds and visa costs are estimates and are labelled as estimates wherever they appear. Always confirm dates on the university’s official page.

      Which countries are covered?

      Belgium, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, the Netherlands, Portugal, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Ukraine, the United Kingdom and the United States.

      What does Elite add, and can I cancel?

      Elite is €15 a year and adds unlimited AI matching, the application assistant and personalised insights. It’s billed yearly and you can cancel anytime.
